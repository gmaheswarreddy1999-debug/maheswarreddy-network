02 // ABOUT ME

Who I Am

I'm a Senior Network Engineer who lives near the routing table. My day-to-day is keeping enterprise networks predictable — whether that means tuning OSPF redistribution at 2am or tightening BGP failover before a go-live window.

I work in Cisco-heavy environments — Catalyst, Nexus, ASR 9000 — and I've spent enough time with packet captures and flow data to trust Wireshark over gut feelings every single time.

On the cloud side I connect DC and AWS together — from VPC design and Security Groups to Terraform-automated builds. If I've done something twice manually, the third time it becomes a playbook or a template.

"I'm the calm one in the remote session during an incident — watching interfaces, routes, and logs, walking teams through small, reversible steps."

I see network engineering as a continuous tuning job, not a one-time build — always circling back to monitor, adjust, and simplify so the next person has less to untangle.
